# BANZA Maintainers

This file records the institutional origin, the maintainer model and the process for adding and removing
maintainers. BANZA is an open financial protocol governed through public repository processes
(see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).

## 1. Institutional origin

Original creator: **BANZAMI - TECNOLOGIA E SERVIÇOS, LDA.**

Creation date: **01/08/2025 (1 de agosto de 2025)** — the historical creation / initial availability date
of the BANZA protocol. This is an institutional/historical attribution; it is **not** a production,
certification, financial-authorisation, active-operator, Trust-Root-issuance or federation date, and it
does not make the original creator an operator, a PSP, or an authority that approves, certifies or
licenses operators.

## 2. Initial institutional maintainer

Initial institutional maintainer: **Banzami**.

## 3. Active maintainers

Active maintainers are recorded through repository ownership and public maintainer records. This file
should be updated as maintainers are added or removed through the public governance process.

## 4. Maintainer responsibilities

- Review pull requests
- Maintain the reference
- Maintain the specifications
- Maintain the tests
- Enforce the financial invariants
- Protect open governance
- Avoid central operator approval
- Preserve the protocol / operator boundary

## 5. Becoming a maintainer

Criteria:

- Sustained contributions
- Technical quality
- Respect for protocol boundaries
- Review participation
- Governance participation
- No conflict with open-protocol principles

## 6. Removing maintainers

Criteria:

- Inactivity
- Harmful conduct
- Conflict of interest
- Breach of governance
- Repeated violation of protocol boundaries

## 7. Banzami role

Banzami may act as institutional maintainer, sponsor, steward and contributor. This does not make every
protocol decision private or closed; protocol governance remains public through the repository.
